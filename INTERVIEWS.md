# Interviews — Scholar Conversations Behind the Dataset

> This file will hold anonymized synthesis from the semi-structured interviews you conducted with admitted Schwarzman Scholars (2020–2027). No transcript is published without explicit scholar consent. Contact via Issues for takedown or attribution.

## How interviews were done

- **Format:** 15–30 min, semi-structured, 3 core questions + follow-ups
- **Core questions:**
  1. Why Schwarzman, why China, why now? (the 4-line test)
  2. How did you frame leadership without sounding like a resume?
  3. What surprised you at Tsinghua vs. expectation?
- **Recruitment:** Outreach via LinkedIn / Schwarzman site + referral, consent recorded
- **Processing:** Notes → summary → scholar reviews → anonymized unless they opt-in to name
- **Ethics:** No inference of ethnicity/religion/politics. Only what they said or published.
